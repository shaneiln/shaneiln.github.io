Frequently asked questions

What is the food like?

The meal will be vegetarian in keeping with our Hindu ceremony. Expect a mix of Indian and Western food, with canapés during cocktail hour followed by a shared-style seated dinner. Please let us know any dietary requirements in your RSVP.

Is the venue indoor or outdoor?

The Glasshouse is an indoor venue, and the whole event will take place indoors.

What is your gift policy?

Your presence is the best gift. If you’d still like to give something, we kindly request no boxed gifts, please.

Will there be photography?

We’d love for everyone to be present and enjoy the evening with us, but you’re welcome to take photos and share them with your friends and family.

What happens during the ceremony?

Our wedding will be a traditional Hindu ceremony of approximately 1 hour 15 minutes. In simple terms, it will follow this order:

  1. Welcome and arrival of the Jaan: the groom and his family are welcomed.
  2. Ganesh Pooja: a prayer to Ganesh, who removes obstacles and brings good fortune.
  3. Arrival of the bride
  4. Commitment: the exchange of garlands.
  5. Sacred fire: the fire acts as witness to the vows.
  6. Seven steps: each step represents a vow we make to one another.
  7. Symbols and close: the exchange of symbols of marriage, then the close of the ceremony.

After the formal ceremony, there will be time for photos before we break for cocktail hour.

What should I wear?

Please see our attire details on the website. In short, wear whatever makes you feel celebratory. Indian and Western attire are both welcome, and nothing is expected.

Is the Glasshouse accessible?

Yes. The Glasshouse has step-free entry, accessible toilets, and accessible parking or drop-off nearby. If you have any access needs, please let us know in your RSVP and we’ll do our best to accommodate them.

What are the details for the event in Spain, and am I invited?

We would love to celebrate with you at both events, but we completely understand that travelling to the other side of the world is a big ask. That’s why we’re holding a celebration in both locations, so loved ones who can’t make the journey can still be part of it.

The Spanish event will be a more laid-back reception and party close to Madrid, sometime during the European spring of 2028. More details will follow.
